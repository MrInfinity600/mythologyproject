01 / Etymology

Greek Names & Linguistic Origins

How Roman deities evolved from Greek counterparts and shaped modern words.

Roman: Mars

Ares — Ἄρης

Mars is the Roman counterpart to the Greek god Ares, leaving a lasting mark on our calendar and solar system.

  • Greek Name: Ares (Ἄρης)
  • Legacy: The planet Mars and the month of March are named after him.
Roman: Minerva

Athena — Ἀθηνᾶ

While equated with the Greek goddess Athena, Minerva's Roman name traces back to ancient Italian roots.

  • Greek Name: Athena (Ἀθηνᾶ)
  • Etymology: Her name comes from the Etruscan goddess Menrva.
02 / Birth Myths

Origins in Roman Mythology

Miraculous and unconventional births from the king and queen of the gods.

Mars • Ἄρης

Offspring of Juno Alone

In one version of Mars' mythology, he was conceived without a father through magical flora.

  • Parentage: Offspring of Juno alone
  • Origin Myth: Juno was given a special flower that made female creatures pregnant without a male, conceiving Mars.
Minerva • Ἀθηνᾶ

Born from Jupiter's Head

The daughter of Jupiter emerged fully grown and ready for battle from her father's skull.

  • Birth Myth: Leapt out of Jupiter's head after it was cut open due to an excruciating headache.
  • Appearance: Emerged wearing armor and a helmet, holding a spear and shield.
